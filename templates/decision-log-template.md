# Decision Log Entry Template

> Standalone copy of the [DECISIONS.md](../DECISIONS.md) format. Use when you want to draft a decision in isolation before appending it to the project's decision log. Append-only — if a decision is reversed, log a new entry that supersedes the old one.

---

## Decision: [Short, specific title — what was decided, not the question]

**Date:** [YYYY-MM-DD]
**Status:** [Active | Superseded by #N | Reverted]

### Context

[2–4 sentences. What's the situation that forced a decision? Why now? What constraints are we operating under?]

### Options considered

1. **[Option name].** [One sentence describing it.]
2. **[Option name].** [One sentence describing it.]
3. **[Option name].** [One sentence describing it.]

> Listing options matters even after the fact. Future-you (or a new collaborator) needs to know what *wasn't* picked, and why.

### Decision made

[The chosen option, stated as an action: "We will X" or "We will not X."]

### Reasoning

- [Reason 1 — concrete, ideally with a tradeoff implied]
- [Reason 2]
- [Reason 3]

### Consequences

- **Good:** [What this enables / makes easier]
- **Bad:** [What this gives up / makes harder]
- **Reversibility:** [How hard would it be to undo this in 6 months? Cheap / Moderate / Expensive]

### Review date

[YYYY-MM-DD — typically 3 to 12 months out, depending on how stable the answer is]
