## New concepts introduced today
- **Stationarity**: statistical properties (mean, variance) of a series don't drift over time. Returns are approximately stationary; prices are not. [FIRST TIME]
- **`>>` delimiter behavior**: treats all whitespace as a separator — spaces, tabs, and newlines. Contrasts with `getline` which reads to `\n` only. [FIRST TIME]
