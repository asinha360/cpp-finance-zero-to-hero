## Concept detail: bond price direction

**Question:** What happens to the bond price when yield rises?

**Initial answer:** "The numerator drops." — Incorrect.

**Correct reasoning:** The coupon payments C and face value F (numerators) are fixed by the bond contract — they don't change. What changes is the denominator: `(1+y)^i` grows when y rises. A larger denominator → smaller PV for each term → lower total bond price.

**Corrected answer:** Bond price falls because the denominator grows, making each discounted cashflow smaller. Direction was correct; mechanism was wrong on first pass.
